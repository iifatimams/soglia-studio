# Phase 0 Discovery: Section A - Brand and Business

## Current Phase

Phase 0: Discovery and Alignment.

## What We Decided

Soglia Studio is a boutique floristry and lifestyle studio launching from a physical store in Muwaileh, Sharjah, UAE. It is not only an ecommerce website. It needs to support a physical retail workflow, online ordering, WhatsApp-assisted orders, delivery, future marketplace channels, and later business modules.

The brand direction is already defined by the brand kit:

- Editorial floristry, not catalogue floristry.
- High-fashion publishing and art-gallery cues.
- Open to customers, but emotionally exclusive.
- Studio-led taste. Soglia chooses with a point of view.
- Slightly playful, without becoming casual or childish.
- English and Arabic must stay separate. No mixed-language copy in one sentence or asset.

## Product Scope

Launch products:

- Flower bouquets.
- Flower bunches.
- Cards.
- Wrapping paper.
- Floral foam / oasis.
- Vases.

Product behavior:

- Bouquets are ready-made arrangements composed by Soglia.
- Flower bunches are simpler stem bundles. Example: one bunch of carnations contains 10 carnations.
- Cards, wrapping paper, vases, and oasis can be sold both as standalone products and as add-ons.

Later products:

- Flower subscriptions.
- Workshops.

Subscription direction:

- Subscriptions come after the first flower catalog is working.
- Subscription customers do not choose the exact weekly flowers.
- Each week they receive something new, either a ready bouquet or a DIY bunch with instruction cards.
- Subscription frequency should support weekly and biweekly plans.

Workshop direction:

- Workshops are planned for a later phase and should not block the MVP.

## Sales Model

Soglia will launch with:

- Physical store in Muwaileh, Sharjah.
- Online bilingual store.
- WhatsApp ordering.
- Third-party delivery.
- Future delivery app channels such as Talabat or similar platforms.

The physical store model is hybrid:

- Customers can walk in.
- Customers can contact Soglia to request a bouquet and pick it up.
- The store should support both retail POS and order-preparation workflows.

## Location, Language, and Currency

Launch location:

- Muwaileh, Sharjah, UAE.

Initial delivery coverage:

- Sharjah.
- Dubai.
- Ajman.

Future delivery coverage:

- Other UAE emirates, once operations are stable.

Languages:

- English.
- Arabic.

Currency:

- AED only.

## Loyalty Program

The store itself is open to everyone.

The invitation-only mechanic applies to the loyalty program, not public purchasing.

Launch loyalty model:

- 8 close friends receive loyalty cards at launch.
- After around 6 bouquet purchases, a loyalty member receives a discounted bouquet or similar reward.
- Each loyalty-card holder can invite one person into the loyalty program.

## Recommended Defaults

The MVP should define Soglia as a Sharjah-based editorial floristry studio with:

- Public ecommerce checkout.
- WhatsApp-assisted ordering.
- Physical POS.
- Pickup.
- Third-party delivery.
- Limited, carefully controlled product range.
- Loyalty as the private layer.

For bouquets, the default should be ready-made or studio-selected arrangements. Customers can choose practical details such as size, occasion, delivery date, pickup, add-ons, and card message. They should not build every bouquet stem by stem in the MVP.

For bunches, the default should be simple inventory-backed stem bundles.

For subscriptions, the default later should be two formats:

- Ready bouquet subscription.
- DIY bunch subscription with instruction cards.

## Risks and Tradeoffs

If bouquets become too customizable, Soglia may start to feel like a normal flower catalogue instead of an editorial studio.

If delivery coverage expands too quickly, quality control may suffer. Flowers are fragile, and the brand promise depends on the object arriving well.

If Arabic is added later as a quick translation layer, the experience will feel weaker. Bilingual content should be planned from the start, even if English content is written first.

If loyalty is too hidden, customers may not understand why it matters. If it is too loud, it may feel gimmicky. The public store should be clear. The loyalty layer should feel earned.

## Open Questions

- Exact reward structure for the loyalty card.
- Whether delivery starts with all three cities at launch or Sharjah first.
- Whether workshops require booking, deposits, waitlists, or private events later.
