# Phase 0 Discovery: Section G - Marketing and Ads

## Current Phase

Phase 0: Discovery and Alignment.

## Summary of Your Answers

Soglia should have marketing and tracking foundations from launch.

TikTok Ads should be used at launch.

Meta Ads should come later.

Google Ads should be used at launch.

Google Analytics 4 should be installed from day one for traffic, ecommerce events, checkout events, and conversion tracking.

Google Search Console should be set up from day one.

Google indexing should be planned from the beginning. This means:

- Search-friendly pages.
- XML sitemap.
- `robots.txt`.
- Clean metadata.
- Search Console submission.
- Indexing checks.
- Product, collection, and content pages that Google can crawl.

Email capture should exist at launch.

You want a blog/community-style page where people can write about:

- The bouquet they received.
- Flowers they wish they received.
- Success stories.
- Flower-related writing.

This content should be moderated before publishing.

Community/blog submissions should not require customer accounts. The submission form should collect the sender's name and phone number for founder records, but the customer can choose to publish anonymously.

Customer-submitted content can include photos.

SMS should be used only for transactional updates.

WhatsApp should be used only for customer support and order updates at launch.

Influencer tracking is not needed at launch. You want to focus first on word of mouth through your own social circle.

Coupon codes should be available at launch, but founder-controlled.

UTM tracking should be supported from launch.

Landing pages should be available from launch. You want strong SEO, and landing pages can help when they target real customer intent.

Campaign profitability reporting is required. The system should eventually connect campaign spend, coupon use, product sales, and gross margin so you can see whether a campaign made money.

## Recommended Default

Marketing MVP should include:

- GA4.
- Google Search Console.
- Google indexing basics.
- UTM capture on orders and analytics events.
- Founder-only coupon code creation.
- Basic campaign records.
- Landing pages.
- Email capture.
- Moderated customer/community content.
- No-account community submissions.
- Optional anonymous publishing.
- Customer-submitted photos.
- Transactional SMS only.
- WhatsApp support and updates only.
- Manual campaign spend entry before full ad integrations.

TikTok Ads and Google Ads can launch early, but the first reporting layer should track essentials:

- Source.
- Medium.
- Campaign.
- Orders.
- Revenue.
- Coupon use.
- Gross margin where available.

## SEO Notes

Landing pages can help SEO if each page has a real purpose.

Good examples:

- Graduation flowers in Sharjah.
- Flower delivery in Muwaileh.
- Weekly bouquet subscription in the UAE.
- Eid flowers in Sharjah.
- Editorial bouquets in Dubai.

Weak examples:

- Dozens of copied city pages with almost the same text.
- Keyword-stuffed pages.
- Thin pages created only for Google.

Soglia should use SEO with taste. Useful pages, specific language, strong metadata, structured data, and real content.

## Risks and Tradeoffs

Coupon codes can cheapen the brand if they become public sale culture. They should feel private, purposeful, or loyalty-linked.

UTM tracking only works if links are used consistently.

TikTok Ads and Google Ads should not run without conversion tracking. Otherwise, spend will be hard to judge.

The community/blog feature needs moderation. Customer-submitted writing should not publish automatically.

SEO takes time. Landing pages help only if they match actual search behavior and do not feel generic.
